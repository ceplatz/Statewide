About us

At AZ Pawn, we don't see a counter between us and our customers. We see neighbors.

For years, people across southeastern Connecticut have trusted us when they needed cash quickly, wanted a fair price for something they own, or were hunting for a great deal. We've earned that trust the old-fashioned way: by listening, by being straight with people, and by treating every customer with respect.

We believe pawning is one of the most practical services a community can have. It's fast, it's private, and it gives you access to money without a credit check. We're proud to provide it, and prouder still of the relationships we've built doing it.

Pawn loans

Cash when you need it. Your item is safe while you get back on track.

A pawn loan lets you borrow against something you own. Bring in your item, we'll appraise it on the spot, and if you accept our offer, you walk out with cash the same day. When you repay the loan, your item comes home to you, exactly as you left it.

No credit checks. No complicated paperwork. No judgment.

What makes us different

Plenty of shops look at an item and name a number. We look a little further. We consider the item, its condition, and the care you've given it, and we also consider you: your history with us and what you need right now. Every loan is a conversation, not a formula.

Your items are in good hands

When you trust us with something you value, we treat it that way. Every pawned item is:

  • Stored in a secure facility
  • Kept in a climate-controlled environment
  • Handled with care and respect from the day it arrives until the day you pick it up

Because we care for items so carefully, we look for the same in what comes through our door. Well-maintained items in good condition earn stronger loan and purchase offers.

Buy & sell

Skip the listings, the haggling, and the no-shows.

Selling something online takes time: photos, postings, messages, strangers at your door. At AZ Pawn, you bring it in, get an honest offer, and leave with cash, often in minutes.

We work hard to pay the best price we can, because we don't want to buy from you once. We want you to come back. Our offers reflect current market value and demand, along with the condition of your item and how well it's been looked after.

Shopping with us? Our showroom is stocked with quality pre-owned merchandise, inspected and fairly priced. Inventory changes daily, so stop by often.

About us

An estate is more than a collection of belongings.

It's a lifetime of memories, milestones, and meaning. When the time comes to settle one, families deserve a partner who understands both its value and its significance.

New England Estate Buyers has helped families through this process for years. We bring deep experience, a broad network, and a genuine commitment to treating every home, and every family, with dignity.

Every estate, every size

No two estates are alike, and we tailor our approach to each one.

We work with everything from a single apartment or modest family home to large, long-established estates with significant collections. Whatever the scope, you receive the same attention, professionalism, and care.

Our services

How we can help.

Full estate purchases

We buy the entire contents of a home outright, giving you one clear, fair offer and a fast resolution.

Estate liquidations & sales

We organize, appraise, and sell contents through the channel best suited to each item.

Auction management

For valuable pieces and collections, we manage auctions to reach the right buyers.

Clean-outs

When a property simply needs to be cleared, we handle it respectfully and efficiently.

Vehicles & other assets

Cars, boats, equipment, and more, valued and sold as part of the estate.

Real estate coordination

Through trusted real estate partners, we help prepare, stage, and present the property itself.

For families who live out of state

We are your eyes, ears, and hands.

Settling an estate from hundreds of miles away is hard. Many of the families we serve can't be here in person, and they trust us to be their eyes, ears, and hands.

We keep you informed at every step, with clear communication, documented inventories, and transparent accounting. Important papers, photographs, and personal keepsakes are set aside for you. You'll always know where things stand, without needing to be in the room.

From contents to closing

We work directly with experienced real estate agents who can clean, stage, and prepare the home for sale. Coordinating the contents and the property together saves time, reduces stress, and helps ensure that everything, from heirlooms to the house itself, achieves its full and fair value.
